Privacy policy

Last updated 10 November 2026

Who we are

Baya By Baker is an online abaya boutique based in Kampala, Uganda. Questions about this policy: orders@bayabybaker.com or WhatsApp +256 755 105402.

What we collect

  • When you order: your name, phone/WhatsApp number, delivery area, any notes, and the pieces in your basket.
  • When you message us: your name, phone and/or email, and your message.
  • When you join our email list: your email address, used only to send you new drops, Eid and holiday sales and your welcome code. Every email lets you unsubscribe, or just reply "stop".
  • Your basket: kept only in your own browser so it is still there when you come back. We cannot see it until you place an order.

How we use it

Only to confirm, deliver and support your order, and to reply to your messages. Your order and messages are sent to our email inbox. If you tap “Send on WhatsApp”, your order goes to us through WhatsApp. We do not sell your details.

Cookies and ads

If you tap Accept on the cookie notice, we use tools from Google (Analytics), Meta (Facebook and Instagram), TikTok and Microsoft (Clarity) to understand which pieces people view and to show our new drops to you on those platforms. If you tap No thanks, none of these are loaded. You can change your choice any time with “Cookie settings” at the bottom of the shop.

How long we keep it

We keep order details for as long as we need them for delivery, exchanges and our records, and then delete them.

Your rights

Under Uganda’s Data Protection and Privacy Act, 2019 you can ask to see the details we hold about you, correct them, or have them deleted. Email orders@bayabybaker.com and we will help.
